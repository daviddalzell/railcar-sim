Clearer Layout Setup: Towns, Tracks & Industries

A user found the terms "Location" and "Switching Area" confusing — it wasn't clear which to create first or how they relate. We're proposing a rename and a nested UI that makes the hierarchy obvious: a Town contains Tracks, and tracks contain Industries.

No data would be lost — this is a rename and a UI reorganisation, not a structural change.

Recently shipped
Oct 2026
Shipped
Session status bar

A persistent bar at the bottom of the screen shows your active session at all times — car count, progress, and a quick link to jump back to it. The dispatcher panel auto-collapses when a session starts so the session is always in view.

Oct 2026
Shipped
Dispatcher conflict panel — Spot & Hold

When two cars compete for the same industry spot, the dispatcher panel now presents a decision UI rather than auto-resolving silently. The system recommends which car to spot based on priority, wait time, and car type — but you make the final call.

  • Spot — commit this car to the industry
  • Hold — park it at the yard until next session
Oct 2026
Shipped
Priority freight

Waybills can now be marked as priority freight (perishable or time-sensitive). Priority cars sort to the top of spotting conflict lists and are highlighted in the dispatcher panel with a priority badge.
