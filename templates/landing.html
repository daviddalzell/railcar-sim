Model Railroad Operations

Operations sessions,
simplified

Waypoint is a web-based platform that brings realistic railroad operations to your model layout. Designed specifically for switching operations on small layouts, it tracks rolling stock, automates waybill routing, and guides every car through prototypical operating sessions — so you spend less time on paperwork and more time running trains.

Secure, multi-operator access

Each layout is hosted on a secure cloud server with a custom web address and password-protected logins. Invite guest operators to connect from their own devices, while you keep full control over permissions and layout access.

AI tools, entirely optional

Waypoint offers optional AI integrations to streamline setup and configuration. All core operational features work seamlessly without AI.

  • Photograph a new car and Waypoint's AI identifies its type, reporting marks, road number, and color. Using your own AI key, you can generate stylized images of rolling stock and locomotives.
  • Generate in and out loads and associate car types for industries based on the industry name.
  • Associate car types with loads using data from real railroad operations. Add your own carload types for operations unique to your layout's region or time period.
  • A free version of Gemini is built in to generate car data from photos and industry data from industry names. Layout owners can optionally add their own API key to generate stylized images of rolling stock. Waypoint supports Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, and local Ollama vision models.

Waybills drive every move

Operations rely on virtual waybills modeled after the classic four-movement car card system used by model railroaders for decades. Waybills automatically route cars based on layout industries and can be updated on the fly.

  • The dispatcher defines train routes with origins, destinations, and intermediate stops. The virtual dispatcher automatically builds consists, assigns motive power, optional cabooses, and operating crews, and assigns train numbers and names.
  • Once a consist is confirmed, live switch lists can be accessed by the operating crew on a phone, tablet, or any device with internet access.
  • Multiple trains can be operated simultaneously by different operators.
  • Off-spot placement (constructive placement) is supported, so cars can be spotted in nearby areas when industry spots are overloaded.
  • Real-time notifications keep dispatchers and operators in sync across devices, while a fast clock runs railroad time at 1×, 2×, 4×, 6×, or 12× speed.
  • At the end of a session, car locations are updated, car waybills are advanced to the next routing, and head end power is released into the pool, ready for the next session.

Any track arrangement

Waypoint can represent any physical track arrangement. Because it is tailored for compact layouts, off-layout staging yards (fiddle yards) are seamlessly integrated into operations. Multi-track yards and traditional staging yards are also fully supported.

  • Designate tracks as industry spurs, storage tracks, yard, or staging. Yard and staging tracks are intended as train origination and destination points.
  • Group tracks into locations, which could be towns, yards, switching areas, and so on.
  • Designate storage or industry tracks as team tracks.
  • Yards and staging support identifying departure and arrival tracks, rip tracks, and so on.
  • Use consist information to assemble cassettes, or pull cars from off-layout storage.
